Apple Canyon Runa golf cart trail

One rugged loop around Apple Canyon Lake — over the dam, across two fork bridges, through backyards and deep pines, past the marina and Orchard Greens, up brutal ridge climbs and under Apple Falls. Free-roam anywhere — but trees, houses, deer and the lake all hit back. Big hits blow the cart sky-high.
